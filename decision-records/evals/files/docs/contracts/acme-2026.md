# Acme — Property Exchange Agreement

## 4.3 Removal of properties

The Supplier shall remove any Property from the Portal within twenty-four (24) hours of
receiving a Removal Instruction. Failure to do so shall constitute a material breach of this
Agreement, and the Supplier shall pay a penalty of two per cent (2%) of the monthly fee for each
month in which the Property remains published.

## 4.4 Moderation

The Portal may reject any Property submitted for moderation. A rejected Property is not published
and the Supplier shall correct it before resubmitting.
